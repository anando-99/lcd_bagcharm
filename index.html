EYES

Eyes

Two blue eyes that look around, blink, follow you as you tilt the charm, and react when you tap them. Pick a mood, or let them choose.

A picked mood lasts ten seconds, then they go back to doing their own thing. On the charm: tap for a reaction, double tap for love, shake to make them dizzy.

JUKEBOX

Jukebox

Tunes the charm plays on its buzzer, sorted into shelves. Play one now, or line a few up and it plays them in order.

    Nothing playing.

    “Listen” plays it here in your browser so you know what you’re getting. The buzzer can only play one note at a time, so these are melodies, not full songs. Pixel originals are new tunes written for the charm in an old-game style.

    Add your own

    Pick a MIDI file of any song you like. The page pulls out the melody, and the charm keeps up to eight of them on the “My songs” shelf.
